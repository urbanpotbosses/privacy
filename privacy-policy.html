DS Dashboard — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 9, 2026

DS Dashboard ("the App") is an internal operations tool built and operated by DS ("we", "us", "our") for the purpose of aggregating and reporting on data from our own restaurant locations. The App is not distributed, sold, or made available to the general public. This Privacy Policy explains what data the App accesses, how it is stored, and how it is used.

1. Who operates this app

DS Dashboard is operated by DS as a private, single-user application used solely for our own business operations. It is not a commercial product or service offered to third parties.

2. What data we access

With explicit authorization from the account owner, the App connects to:

  • Toast — point-of-sale order, check, and sales category data for our restaurant locations.
  • 7shifts — employee scheduling, time-punch, and wage data for our restaurant locations.
  • QuickBooks Online — accounting data (income, expenses, and related reports) for our business.

No data belonging to any other business, customer, or individual outside of our own operations and employees is accessed.

3. How data is stored

All synchronized data and connection credentials (API keys, OAuth tokens, and refresh tokens) are stored locally in an encrypted database on a device controlled by the business owner. Credentials are encrypted at rest using AES-256-GCM and are only decrypted in memory after the authorized user enters a personal passphrase. The App does not transmit data to any cloud service, analytics provider, or third party other than the originating platforms (Toast, 7shifts, QuickBooks Online / Intuit) required to retrieve the data itself.

4. How data is used

Data retrieved from these sources is used exclusively to:

  • Generate internal sales, labor, and financial reports for our own locations.
  • Track labor cost against internally set targets.
  • Support internal, owner-facing analysis of potential future restaurant locations using our own historical operating data.

Data is never sold, rented, or shared with advertisers, data brokers, or any other third party.

5. Data retention and deletion

Synchronized data is retained locally for as long as needed for internal reporting and historical comparison. The owner may delete the local database, or disconnect and revoke API/OAuth access via Toast, 7shifts, or Intuit's own account management tools, at any time.

6. Third-party services

Use of Toast, 7shifts, and QuickBooks Online data is subject to each provider's own terms of service and privacy policy, in addition to this policy:

  • Toast Privacy Policy
  • 7shifts Privacy Policy
  • Intuit Privacy Statement

7. Security

Credentials are encrypted at rest and only decrypted in memory for the duration of an authorized local session. The App is not exposed on the public internet; it runs locally for use by the business owner.

8. Children's privacy

The App is an internal business tool and is not directed at, or used by, children.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the App's functionality changes. The effective date above reflects the most recent revision.
